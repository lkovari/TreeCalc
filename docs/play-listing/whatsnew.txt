EN (492 characters)

The tree screen plays an evaluation back. The full formula sits above postfix, in small type, and highlights the step in progress. Step by step turns Play into Next: one operation, then it stops. Stop clears the highlight. With the switch off, Play walks every step and Pause holds. Operations lists each step. Tap a row or a postfix chip to jump; the tree highlights that branch. Help explains playback and how infix becomes postfix. About notes this is one way a calculator can work inside.
